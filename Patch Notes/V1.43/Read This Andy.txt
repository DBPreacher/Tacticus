‘Terminus’ Survival event - October 10 to October 16
Take command of Lord Marshal Dreir and hold the line across 20 waves. All non-Space Marine Imperial characters and the Ultramarines are eligible for deployment. No Machines of War can be deployed in this event.
NEW: Astra Militarum Guardsmen can be deployed in Survival battle by spending Forces.
-- Each Guardsman you deploy raises the cost of the next one and the cost resets every time you move up a rarity.
-- Up to 20 Guardsmen can be deployed per rarity.
Earn additional Forces by completing the event's daily missions.
This event has no featured character. Instead, the 35 event crates contain Crusade resources, Coins and XP Books.
This event runs in parallel with the final week of Domination gameplay
No offer calendar this time — a new deal is available each day for the duration of the event.
‘Against the Tide’ returns October 10 to October 16
Gain event points for each enemy wave you defeat
Collect many resources for Crusade and Terminus:
-- Seasonal Event Currency
-- Crusade Credits
-- Crusade Ordnance
-- Intel
-- Shards for random units
