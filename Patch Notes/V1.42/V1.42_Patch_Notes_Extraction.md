### The Great Awakening — September 12–18

- A Necron tomb world awakens; Adeptus Astartes seek the relic at its heart. Command Thothmek against successive Space Marine waves.
- All Xenos characters eligible; no Machines of War.
- Last Stand ruleset returns: hold key ground and receive the tomb world's support. Collect battlefield reinforcements to summon Necron support units as assaults intensify.
- Mission chains award Event Points and a Thothmek stat boost in Survival.
- Crates contain Kîmm shards, Xenos upgrades, Ability Badges, Coins and Forge Badges.
- **35 crates total:** the normal first 30 remain, with the exclusive custom event theme in crate 30; five additional bonus crates provide stretch goals.
- Free and paid bundles in the event offer calendar. Against the Tide runs alongside it and supplies event currency.

### Terminus — October 10–16 (text-only schedule)

- Command Lord Marshal Dreir across 20 waves. Eligible: all non-Space Marine Imperial characters, plus Ultramarines. No Machines of War.
- New: spend Forces to deploy Astra Militarum Guardsmen. Each deployed Guardsman increases the next one's cost; cost resets on moving up a rarity. Maximum 20 Guardsmen per rarity.
- Daily missions supply additional Forces.
- No featured reward character; Dreir is the commanded unit. All 35 event crates instead contain Crusade resources, Coins and XP Books.
- Runs alongside the final week of Domination gameplay. Event/daily offers, with a new deal each day; no offer calendar.
- Parallel Against the Tide awards points per enemy wave defeated and provides Seasonal Event Currency for Terminus, Crusade Credits, Crusade Ordnance, Intel and random character shards. Requires Player Level 20+.

## Monthly Improvements

### Characters and traits

- **Prioritised Efficiency — reworked:** unit deals +25% Damage with all attacks and takes −25% Damage. Traversing three or more hexes during its turn removes the effect until the start of its next turn.
- **Movement 2 → 3:** Vynn, Hâvyr, Âmmuk, Ûthar the Destined and Ironkin Steeljack (NPC).
- **Ûthar — Ancestral Fortune:** deals 3× Plasma Damage to an adjacent enemy and Taunts it for one round. Target cannot be revived, healed, repaired or otherwise regain Health for one round. Ûthar gains +X% Block chance until his next turn starts. After a combined total of Y attacks that he has blocked or scored Crits in after using it, the ability becomes usable again. X/Y placeholders are retained as approved; exact scaling values are not needed.
- **Ûthar — Grim Efficiency:** switches between stances affecting himself and friendly units within two hexes. Fortify Takeover makes incoming attacks pass through Armour value X an additional time. Hostile Acquisition makes affected units ignore Y Armour with all attacks. Stance switches when Ûthar uses his active or traverses at least three hexes during his turn. X/Y placeholders are retained as approved; exact scaling values are not needed.
- **Thrill Seekers:** now becomes Thrilled on losing Health, rather than on being attacked; rest unchanged.
- **Healing prevention:** “cannot be healed” effects (example: Mephiston's Fury of the Ancients) now also prevent special heal/repair actions that grant extra benefits. Affected units listed: Actus, Hollan, Baldr and Vynn.

### Modes and features

- **Tournament Arena Faction War:** Necron Strategic Reserves NPCs changed to Deathmarks.
- **Guild Projects — new Planetary Defenses:** activate only during Crusade Domination. Progress by donating Intel or defeating non-summoned enemies with Overwatch units in Arena, Onslaught and Salvage Run. Completion grants one use of the Planetary Defenses guild action. Action is Domination-only, on planets owned by the guild's side; raises the enemy score required to capture that planet, applying to all future enemy captures until Crusade season end.
- **Guild Projects — Orbital Bombardment task:** non-summoned enemy kills using Flame, Blast or Melta Damage now count; previously Flame only.
- **Guild actions — targeting:** Guild Leaders and Co-Leaders may use Orbital Bombardment and Mass Reinforcements on the planet they are targeting. It may be the Guild Target but no longer must be.
- **Guild Raids — effective Season 110, September 23:** Mythic NPC Damage and Armour reduced approximately 8% at Mythic I, 10% at Mythic II, 12% at Mythic III. Applies to enemy units present at battle start, not bosses themselves.
- **Guild Raids — Kill Team/Side Boss Health:** unified at 1,440 at Common I, scaling upward across tiers. **Corrodius and Thaumachus remain unchanged**.
- **Guild Raids — conditional boss-damage abilities:** adjusted for new Health and design goals; examples supplied: Boss Adjutant, Vulnerable to Melee, Reinforcements, Phantasm, etc.
- **Magnus — Treason of Tzeentch:** original character stays in its hex when the copy is created; copy appears on a random hex within three hexes of Magnus. When a character attacks Magnus while its clone is present, character and clone swap positions.
- **Magnus — AI:** adjustments to use frequency/selection of Treason of Tzeentch and Blade of Magnus, and their targets. Developer intent: maintain challenge while giving players more agency rather than unavoidable displacement.
- **Pathfinding:** improved avoidance of hexes a Guild Boss ability will damage at the start of the next turn.
- **Squig Smash rework:** three initial Squigs, two per new wave, no bounce on hit; complete modes/rewards in Calendar.
- **Crusade Vault offer:** warning on attempting purchase when Vault is below 50% full.
- **Operations Speed Ups:** two hours of free Speed Ups daily from the Crusade Shop. Each item reduces an Operation's time by one hour. Can accelerate completion to reuse a unit in another Operation or be saved for later use.
- **Operations:** “claim all operations” button added.

No new relic kit is supplied; Nether-Realm Casket is an event reward and Talons of Horus/Phoenix Gem are shop rotation content.
