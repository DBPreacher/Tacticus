Guild Projects:
Added new Project: Planetary Defenses
-- Can only be activated during the Domination Phase in Crusade. To complete this Project, donate Intel or defeat enemy units (except summons) with Overwatch units in Arena, Onslaught and Salvage Run. When completed, grants one use of the Guild action “Planetary Defenses”.
-- The Guild action can only be used during the Domination Phase of Crusade, and only on planets currently owned by the Guild’s side. When used, it increases the score needed by the enemy side to successfully capture the planet. This applies to all future (enemy) captures until the end of the Crusade season.

Adjusted task for Project: Orbital Bombardment
-- The task can now be progressed by defeating enemy units (except summons) with Flame, Blast, and Melta Damage (previously was only Flame Damage).

Targeting rules for Orbital Bombardment and Mass Reinforcements updated:
-- Can now be used by Guild Leaders and Co-Leaders on the planet they are targeting. This can be the Guild Target, but no longer has to be the Guild Target.

New Guild War season starting September 16
Guild Raids - Changes effective with Season 110 on 23 September
Mythic NPCs have had their Damage and Armour scaled down as follows:
Mythic I: decreased ~8%
Mythic II: decreased ~10%
Mythic III: decreased ~12%

This change applies to enemy units that start the battle on the battlefield, but not bosses themselves

Kill Team Bosses (aka Side Bosses) at all tiers have had their Health unified to 1440 at Common I (and scaling up accordingly from there) with two notable exceptions
-- Rotbone and Thaumachus both remain unchanged

Kill Team Boss abilities that conditionally damage the boss have been adjusted to reflect new Health values and better align with boss design goals (Boss Adjutant, Vulnerable to Melee, Reinforcements, Phantasm, etc.)

Magnus the Red:
-- Treason of Tzeentch changed:
---- The original character remains in its original hex instead of being teleported when the copy is created. The copy now appears in a random hex within 3 hexes of Magnus. 
---- New effect: When a character attacks Magnus while the clone of that character is on the battlefield, the character and the clone swap places.

-- Several adjustments to AI behaviour and ability selection, related to how often Magnus uses Treason of Tzeentch and Blade of Magnus, and which units he targets with these abilities. 

Developer note: After these changes, the fight should still feel challenging and befitting of the Daemon Primarch of Tzeentch, but it should give players a bit more agency in their decisions rather than them just reacting to circumstances out of their control (like units being teleported without a chance to avoid it).

Balancing:
Leagues of Votann have seen some adjustments to their Faction Trait, Movement stat and in the case of Ûthar, also to abilities:

-- Prioritised Efficiency has been reworked. New effect:
This unit deals +25% Damage with all attacks and takes -25% Damage. If it traverses 3 or more hexes during its turn, it loses this effect until the start of its next turn.

-- Vynn, Hâvyr, Âmmuk, Ûthar the Destined, Ironkin Steeljack (NPC): Movement increased 2 -> 3

-- Ûthar the Destined
---- Ancestral Fortune has been reworked. New effect:
Deals 3x Plasma Damage to an adjacent enemy and Taunts it for 1 round. The targeted unit cannot be revived, healed, repaired, nor otherwise restored Health for 1 round. Ûthar also gains +X% Block chance until the start of his next turn. After a combined total of Y attacks that Ûthar has blocked or scored Crits in after using Ancestral Fortune, the ability can be used again.
---- Grim Efficiency has been reworked. New effect:
Ûthar switches between two stances that affect him and all friendly units within 2 hexes:
Fortify Takeover: Attacks against affected units have to go through an Armour value of X an additional time.
Hostile Acquisition: Affected units ignore Y Armour with all attacks
The stance switches whenever Ûthar uses his active ability or traverses 3 or more hexes during his turn.

Thrill Seekers: Units with this trait now become Thrilled when they lose Health instead of when they are attacked. (The rest of the trait does not change)

Units that “cannot be healed”, for example from being under the effects of Mephiston’s Fury of the Ancients, can no longer be healed or repaired by special heal or repair actions that also grant an additional benefit. Affected units:
Actus
Hollan
Baldr
Vynn

Improvements:
Improved pathfinding behaviour related to hexes that will be damaged by a Guild Boss ability at the start of the next turn. Units should be better at avoiding these hexes now.

Correcting the mission task for Lucius’ first mythic mission to clarify that the enemies must be killed by the Armour of Shrieking Souls effect (not just killed while under the effect)
