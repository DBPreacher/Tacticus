## Overview

- Release note: **Apple is delayed until tomorrow**; Android rollout has "just started" — a slow start given the size of this update.
- **Crusade** — brand new mode, launches **August 8**. Pick a Faction side, join a worldwide conflict.
  - Complements existing modes: battles in Campaign, Campaign Events, Arena, Guild Raids, and Guild War all contribute to conquering worlds for your Faction.
  - Earn rewards for Securing/Devastating worlds, plus season-total rewards across a **10-week season**.
  - Requirements to participate: **Player Level 20+**, **Guild Level 5+**.
  - Explicitly an early-version feature release — Snowprint is soliciting feedback on what to add/fix/adjust next.
  - **Operations**: dispatch characters (matching rarity/rank/Grand Alliance requirements, plus bonus objectives for traits/damage types) for a timed return with rewards. Dispatched characters remain usable elsewhere. Rewards include Crusade resources, Guild Project resources, and XP. New Operations requested daily; Operation Board can be leveled up for better Operations.
  - **Guild Projects**: Guild-wide projects, progressed via donating resources (notably new resource **Intel**) or project-specific tasks. Completing one rewards the Guild (Project reward + Guild XP) and contributing members (Coins). Up to **2 active Projects** at once. Two projects available at launch: **Orbital Bombardment** and **Mass Reinforcements** (more to come). Both grant a special Crusade action use, and can be completed multiple times per season at increasing cost.
- **"War Amongst the Stars"** — Tacticus' 4th Anniversary event, **two-week celebration from August 5**.
  - Two weekly Shop Event phases, each with its own Home Screen Events (Training Rush week 1, then Arsenal of War + Conflict Operations from week 2).
  - Rotating daily deals in the Event Shop, tailored to power level/roster, purchased with new currency **War Orders**.
  - War Orders earned via missions, event milestones, Tournament Arena crates, Cezare's Character Quest, Home Screen Event rewards, and free calendar offers.
  - Daily War Order spend unlocks Shards (all current Requisition units), Orbs, Coins; from week 2, also Intel/Forces/Ordnance (Crusade fuel).
  - Progress bar fills as War Orders are spent, unlocking further rewards.
  - **Balance note**: fewer War Orders required to clear all crates this time vs. past events, and milestone War Order rewards reduced accordingly — but rebalanced so higher milestones reward disproportionately more than lower ones (rewards the most engaged players).
  - **August 15 (anniversary day)**: exclusive free offer (4th Anniversary Frame) + separate paid offer (4th Anniversary Avatar).
- New Battle Pass season starts **August 2**, featuring **Ramus** (see Characters section) — first Rare to debut via Battle Pass. Also unlockable instantly via a promo code included with the *Tacticus – The Miniatures Game* board game (available now at Target US; coming to Germany/Australia).

## Monthly Improvements

**New section this patch** — everything that isn't a new character, a calendar event, or a requisition, but was still sitting uncategorized in earlier drafts of this doc. Built into its own HTML slide with two categories:

**New Relics:**
- **Helspear** (Haarken Worldclaimer) — Mythic Crit Item, +35% Crit Chance / +1050 Crit Damage. Herald of the Apocalypse always adds Despoiled Ground to affected enemies' hexes, and the next attack against them ignores 685 Armour. Reusable again once Haarken defeats 8 enemies after using it.
- **Phasal Subjugator** (Anuphet) — Mythic Crit Booster Item, +6% Crit Chance / +350 Crit Damage. After moving (or at end of turn if he doesn't move), summons a Necron Warrior nearby if none are on the field; triggers a second time after "My Will Be Done," letting another unaffected adjacent Necron Warrior attack too. Summoned Warriors: 5875 Health, 1960 Damage, 1470 Armour.
- **Anuphet's passive rework — "My Will Be Done"** (directly tied to the relic above): at the end of his turn, a friendly adjacent Mechanical unit now performs a normal attack against a target in range, using 90% of its own Damage stat, up to a cap. If no attack was performed this way, Anuphet instead gains a temporary Damage bonus until the end of his next turn.

**Mode & Feature Changes:**
- **Tournament Arena Faction War** — Cezare now deployed with the Blood Angels instead of Sword Brother Godswyl.
- **Incursion — Tau Broadside Battlesuit revamp** (Aug 24–29) — branching paths added to Incursion tiers, a new Enhancement set (Infiltrate, Heal, Damage Boost, Pierce Boost, Summon: Inceptor), and rebalanced battle difficulty to match.
- **Guild War** — full reward/scoring rework:
  - End-of-Season chests (leaderboard reward): +100%
  - Per battle (all Battlefield Levels): +100%
  - Per kill (all Battlefield Levels): +66%
  - Per win — BF5 +250% · BF4 +133% · BF3 +50% · no change on BF1–2
  - Score multiplier — BF5 factor 4→8 · BF4 factor 3→4 · no change on BF1–3
