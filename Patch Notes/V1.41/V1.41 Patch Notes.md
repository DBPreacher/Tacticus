NEW FEATURE: Crusade – The galaxy is at war. Will your Guild leave its mark? 

Crusade is an all-new mode designed to complement your normal gameplay. Fight where you normally would - but now every battle has a greater purpose: As you complete battles across existing game modes (Campaign, Campaign Events, Arena, Guild Raids, Guild War), you'll contribute to conquering worlds for your Faction across the galaxy.

Earn rewards for every world you contribute to Securing or Devastating and for your total achievements throughout the 10-week Crusade season.
Engage in Operations and Guild Projects (see below) to further your objectives
Requirement to participate in Crusade: Player Level 20 or higher, Guild Level 5 or higher.

As always, we release an early version of the feature and rely on your feedback to determine how it develops. What should we add, fix or adjust next?  Check out the feature release post in game for more details, and join the discussion on Discord!
Operations
Dispatch your characters across the galaxy and receive valuable rewards upon their return!
Select a team of characters that fit the requirements of the Operation, including rarity, rank and Grand Alliance. Fulfil a number of bonus objectives, like traits and damage types, to receive bonus rewards.
Once dispatched, each Operation takes an amount of time to complete before rewards can be claimed. Dispatched characters are free to use in all other game modes.
Rewards include resources for Crusade and Guild Projects, as well as XP for the dispatched characters.
Request new Operations each day and level up your Operation Board to get access to more valuable Operations.

Guild Projects
Work together with your Guild to progress and complete Projects! Completing a Project gives the Guild a specific Project-related reward as well as Guild XP, while members that participated earn Coins
Projects can be progressed by donating resources, most notably the new resource Intel, or alternatively by contributing to Project specific tasks (similar to missions)
Guilds can have up to two Projects active at the same time
(Initially there are two available Projects: Orbital Bombardment and Mass Reinforcements, with more Projects to be added over time)
Both Projects grant one use of a special action in the new Crusade game mode
Both Projects can be completed several times per Crusade season, with the cost increasing each time they are completed

All deals in the Event Shop are customized based on your power level and unit collection and can be claimed with the event's special currency: War Orders.
-- Complete the event's and Battle Pass missions to gain Event Points and reach the event's milestones.
-- Collect War Orders by completing missions, reaching the event's milestones, opening crates in the Tournament Arena and Cezare's Character Quest, and claiming rewards in Home Screen Events and the free calendar offers.
-- Each day, you can use War Orders to claim Shards (featuring all the units currently available from Requisitions for all players), Orbs, and Coins. ---- From week two, additional deals unlock offering Intel, Forces, and Ordnance to fuel your Crusade.
-- A progress bar fills up as you use your War Orders to claim the event's deals, unlocking even more rewards along the way!

Note: Compared to previous events, we have reduced the number of War Orders required to claim all crates. We have also consequently reduced the War Orders rewarded by the event milestones. Higher milestones now reward more War Orders than lower ones, to reward the most engaged players.

A dedicated offer calendar runs throughout the event, and on the anniversary day, August 15, celebrate with an exclusive free offer featuring the 4th Anniversary Frame, plus a special offer featuring the 4th Anniversary Avatar.
