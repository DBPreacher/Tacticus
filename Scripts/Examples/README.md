# Example scripts

Split in two because they are used for two different things, and mixing them up
is how the tool's style gets written into the voice guide by mistake.

## `His own writing/` — the voice reference

Fifteen scripts the owner wrote himself. **Quote the style from these, never from
the other folder.** Measured across them:

| Kind of video | Words per sentence (median) | Longest 10% | Em dashes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discursive — Top 10 countdowns, "get X for free" guides | 24–27 | 39–58 | **zero** |
| Explanatory — character reviews, beginner guides, analysis | 15–19 | 33–42 | **zero** |

Zero em dashes in every single one, across both 2024 and 2025. That is the
sharpest single tell.

Two of these earn a special mention:

- **`New charts showing the best characters`** is the **structure reference**. It
  is the newest script and the only one using the current shape — Hook /
  Introduction / parts / Outro. Every other script here still opens with the
  older `Part 1 - The Problem`, which he has moved away from. It began as a Claude
  draft that he rewrote, so use it for structure and for what his edit changed,
  and use the others for sentence style.
- **`Does Wrask do anything_`** is the shortest complete video here — 1,598 spoken
  words, 8.3 minutes, eight sections. Proof that a tight eight minutes is a
  finished script rather than one that needs padding.

## `Tool assisted/` — structure only

Nine scripts written with a tool's help. **The voice is not his in these.** They
are kept because the running order and section breakdown are still useful, and
because the contrast is the most reliable way to spot the tool's habits:

- em dashes, which he never uses — 33 in each `Plan NOW to get…`, 18 in Laviscus
- short, tidy sentences — median 13–14 against his 15–27
- dramatic one-line paragraphs: *"Death is not an end for Lucius. It is a door."*
- three-part lists in every other sentence

`Lysander Best Deep Strike Teams` and `Patch 1.42` are the newest here and do use
the current Hook / Introduction structure, so they are the best structural
examples in this folder.

A useful pair to compare directly: **`Do this NOW to get Mephiston for FREE`** (his,
zero em dashes) against **`Plan NOW to get Uthar for FREE!`** and **`Plan NOW to get
Lysander for FREE!`** (tool, 33 em dashes each, and statistically identical to one
another — one is a template copy of the other). Same format, same job, three very
different voices.
