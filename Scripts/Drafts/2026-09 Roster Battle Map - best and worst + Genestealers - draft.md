## The best and worst characters

[On screen: Progression **Diamond III**, Ability level **36**, Active ability **On**, Gear **Standard**, then click **Attack**]

So now that you know how to read it, let's get to the bit I know a lot of you have been waiting for, which is who actually comes out on top. For all of this I'm using Diamond III characters with level 36 abilities, their gear on and their active switched on. That's a lot closer to how most of us actually play them than a character standing there with no equipment. And just remember that the middle of the roster at this setting is about 3 attacks for both damage and toughness, so anyone well below that for damage, or well above it for toughness, is doing something really special.

Right at the top of the damage pile we have Thutmose and Kharn, who both kill a typical character in just 1.6 attacks. Kharn won't surprise anyone, because his passive throws four extra hits onto every single attack. But Thutmose is the one that really caught my eye, because every attack he makes brings a bolt of Direct damage with it, and Direct damage doesn't care how much armour you've got. Just behind them are Commander Farsight, who follows every melee attack with a free ranged one, then Mephiston and Thaddeus Noble.

Mephiston is the one I want to point out here, because with no gear he's only 32nd. But give him the crit items he'd actually have and he shoots all the way up to 4th, which is a pretty good sign you should be gearing him properly if you've been putting it off.

[On screen: scroll to the bottom of the Attack view]

Down at the bottom, we've already met Ammuk, and even with his gear and his active he still needs over 13 attacks. He really is just that bad at hitting people on his own. Then we have Bellator, Isabella and Gibbascrapz, and what they've all got in common is that their active is a summon, which remember, the map doesn't count. So they're not bad characters, they just get other things to do the fighting for them.

[On screen: click **Defence**]

Over on toughness, top of the pile by quite a distance is Aesoth, who takes 8.5 attacks to bring down. That's nearly three times what a typical character manages, and it's all down to his active giving him 66% damage reduction for the turn. Then we have Lysander, Tyrant Guard, Trajann and Brother Burchard.

Lysander and Trajann are the two that really come alive here, because both of their kits lean on blocking. So once they've got their gear and their active going, Lysander climbs from 17th to 2nd and Trajann from 25th to 4th. And yes, our old friend the clobster is still sitting in 3rd, which makes me very happy.

At the bottom, Xybia is dead last, and we'll come back to her in a minute. But Lucien in 116th is a really interesting one, because he's only down there because of his active. Black Rage drops him to half his health, so the moment you switch it on he falls from 70th to 116th. It's a good reminder that some of these actives really do come with a cost. Macer is the same story, because his Jakhal Stimms cost him a quarter of his health, and he has the lowest armour in the whole game to begin with.

[On screen: click **Map**]

And on the map, the characters furthest into that top right corner are Nubari, Sword Brother Godswyl and Mephiston. They're all top 10 for damage while still being in the top third for toughness. Then Aesoth, Uthar and Hascule come at it from the other direction, because they're really hard to kill but still hit harder than most.

If you want a proper glass cannon, then look at Thaddeus Noble up in the top left, who's 5th for damage but 108th for toughness. He'll delete almost anything, but you really need to keep him out of reach. And the clobster is our tank in the bottom right, 3rd toughest but only 95th for damage, which honestly is exactly what I want from him.

## My favourite faction

[On screen: all three alliances back on, then type "Genestealer" in **Find**]

Now you all know that the Genestealer Cults are my favourite faction, and if you sat through all 37 minutes of my beginner's guide then you definitely know it. So I really wanted to see where they landed, and honestly, it's not pretty.

They're the squishiest faction in the game by a mile. The typical Genestealer sits at 113th out of 117 for toughness, and the next worst faction, the Thousand Sons, are all the way back at 98th. Xybia is dead last in every single setting I tried, and she and Hollan have the joint lowest health in the game, so they fold like a deckchair the moment anything looks at them. Hollan is right behind her in 115th, with Judh in 113th and Isaak in 105th.

- **CREED:** *(from off screen)* Finally, a faction I could actually beat.
- **ME:** You're retired, Creed.
- **CREED:** Retired, not dead.

The only one who really holds his own is the Patermine, who sits in 40th for both damage and toughness. And Judh, who we saw earlier, is a proper damage dealer once she's got her gear, but she still goes down in under 2 attacks.

So why is my favourite faction getting absolutely hammered? Well, it's because nearly everything they're good at is exactly the stuff I told you the map doesn't count. Hollan is a healer whose active is a summon, Isaak and Xybia are all about placing decoys and turning them into Neophyte Hybrids, and even the Patermine's active summons Purestrain Genestealers onto his decoys. So on their own they're some of the weakest characters in the game. But as a cult working together they're a completely different beast, and that's exactly why I'll always tell you when a character's real strength is somewhere the map can't see.
