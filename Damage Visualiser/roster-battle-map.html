Reading the chart

Damage: higher is better
How many of their attacks it takes to kill a typical enemy. The middle result against all characters.
Toughness: further right is better
How many attacks a typical enemy needs to kill them. Terminator Armour cuts only the first attack of the turn.
Always-on traits
Only traits that always work: Terminator Armour, Mk X Gravis, Parry, Terrifying, Martial Ka'tah, Camouflage (−1 hit), Beast Slayer.
All triggered
Every situational trait works: didn't move, first attack of the battle, ranged character starts away from enemies, Khorne at 4 kills, Nurgle aura on melee targets. Random traits (Daemon, Get Stuck In, Let the Galaxy Burn) count at their average.
Active ability
Everyone opens with their active if it beats a normal attack, then carries on with normal attacks. Damage to other enemies, summons and buffs don't count, and defensive actives aren't modelled yet. At Winged D3 a level 36 active rarely beats a normal attack; at level 50 most do.

Who is where
Stats are from the tacticustable.com game data at Winged D3 with no gear. The rules are in Damage Visualiser/DAMAGE_MODEL.md and each active ability's handling is in active_abilities.csv. The numbers are for comparing characters, not predicting an exact fight.
