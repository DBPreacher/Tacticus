Reading the Support Map

What it measures
For every ally who can use a buff, how much faster they kill a typical character with it: the same model and settings as the Roster Battle Map. +50% means they do half as much again.
Boost per ally: higher is better
The middle of those boosts, among the allies it actually helps.
Across: allies reached (the default)
How many of your four teammates it reaches in one turn, from its range and the Team spacing switch. Dots in a column are spread sideways so their names fit. The dashed curves are team boost: anything on the same curve is worth the same to your team.
Across: can use it
How many of the other 116 characters it improves at all: a roster-building view. A Blood Angels-only buff can be big but narrow.
Team boost
Boost per ally × allies reached. 100% is like having one more typical ally hitting. It assumes a team built to use the buff; "can use it" shows how narrow that team is.
Team spacing
How many teammates an aura reaches. Typical: adjacent 2, within 2 hexes 3. Tight: 3 and 4. Spread: 1 and 2. Team-wide buffs and debuffs on the enemy reach all 4; one-ally and next-attack buffs reach 1.
Against some enemies only
Buffs that only work against Psykers, Daemons or Chaos are scored across the whole roster: +50% against 2 Daemons out of 117 counts as about +1%. The panel shows the full figure too.
What counts
Conditional parts follow the Traits switch, actives the Active switch, relics Mythic with gear. +Damage buffs work on normal attacks (ability damage doesn't use the Damage stat); debuffs on the enemy reach actives too. Not counted: summons, Overwatch, movement, and the support's own damage (that's on the Roster Battle Map).
Coming next
The Defence side: heals, damage reduction, taunts and control.
