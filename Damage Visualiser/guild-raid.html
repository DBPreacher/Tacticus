    Reading the chart

    What a raid attack is
    Five characters and one Machine of War against a boss that cannot die inside your attack — you are chipping at a health bar the whole guild shares. Six rounds, but the first goes on getting into position, so the model counts 5 rounds of fighting. The boss's own faction is banned, so no Death Guard against Mortarion.
    The setup, and why it isn't a set of switches
    Every number here is at one setting: . That is what a maxed roster looks like today, and it is what the videos are recorded at. Earlier versions of this page let you flip the level, the gear and the triggers, which meant working out thousands of answers and searching each one shallowly. One setting means the search can be thorough instead.
    Standard gear
    Each character's own item slots filled with the best Mythic items they can equip, at top level, boosters included — and the character's relic in its slot at level 10, with its effect counted. Gear is where crit chance and Crit Damage come from, so it changes who is worth bringing, not just how hard they hit.
    ▲ High ground
    +50% Damage to whoever is standing on it. The model gives it to the two characters already doing the most damage in that five, which is what the teams in the videos do — they are marked ▲. It counts for everything they deal: their weapon, the hits their passive adds, and their active.
    Side battles cleared
    The two smaller fights beside the boss. Clearing them takes Armour off the boss and softens its block, and it weakens some bosses' own rules — Mortarion's Revoltingly Resilient lets three hits through at full strength instead of one. The panel on the right shows the Armour it is left with, and what it was before.
    All triggered
    Every trait whose condition is met counts: Rapid Assault, Crushing Strike, Heavy Weapon, Ranged Specialist and the rest. Over five rounds against one enormous target, most of them do fire — this is the optimistic reading, not a guaranteed one.
    Actives on
    Everyone uses their active. Most are once a battle; the few with a cooldown come back as often as it allows. An active that grows through the fight (Kariyan, Atlacoya) is held back as late as it can be without losing a use. On the turn it goes off, the model takes whichever is bigger — the active or a normal attack.
    The bars
    The solid part is what that character would do alone against this boss. The faded part is what the other four and the Machine of War add — buffs, debuffs on the boss, and Laviscus's Outrage, which feeds on his team-mates' biggest hits. A character with a short solid bar and a long faded one is not carrying the team; the team is carrying them.
    Machine of War
    The sixth slot. Each machine has one free ability and one that spends a munition, and it fires every round once its cooldown is up — munitions are not the limit in a raid. Its own damage counts, and so does the Mythic ability many of them have, which usually makes the boss take more damage. Its faction is banned the same way your characters' is.
    Bosses are Immune
    Nothing reduces a boss's Armour and it cannot be Stunned, Suppressed or Taunted — so characters built around those do nothing here, however good they are elsewhere. Bosses are also Big Targets, which some abilities hit harder.
    The best five there is
    Not "the best we found". Every possible five was scored — 2.1 billion teams across these fights — so no other five does more. Earlier versions built a team a character at a time and then tried swapping one out, which is fast but can settle for a team it cannot improve by any single swap while a completely different five is better. On half these fights it did exactly that, by as much as 9%.
    Who else fits
    The most that character can manage on this boss, in the best five they can be part of — and the gap to the best five overall. Every team containing them was scored, so it is their ceiling, not an estimate. Hover to see the five they would be in. Useful when you don't own one of the five above: it says who to build around instead, and what it costs you.
    Try your own five
    The picker below runs the same model in your browser on any five you name, at the same setting and the same boss, so you can test the team you actually own against the best one there is. It checks itself against exact scores from the model before it shows a number.
    What is not counted
    The boss killing your characters — every five here fights all five rounds. The movement round. Where anyone is standing, beyond the high ground. Bombs and Overwatch, which don't count towards a raid score and nobody uses. Against the owner's own recorded runs the model lands about 6–8% low, and most of that looks like damage scored in the round it treats as movement.
